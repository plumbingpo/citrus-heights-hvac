Kitchen Climate Control

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Citrus Heights HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and guests comfortable throughout.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Citrus Heights HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Citrus Heights and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on rooftop and split systems. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to prevent a repeat, and every response is fully documented for your records.
